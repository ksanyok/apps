How it works

Pick a topic and go through the flashcards: tap a card to see the translation (Ukrainian, Russian or English — choose in Settings), tap the speaker to hear the pronunciation, then rate how well you knew the word. Spaced repetition brings each word back right before you would forget it. "Review now" on the home screen collects every word that is due today.

Polski Krok Pro

Polski Krok Pro is an auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly) with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. It unlocks all topics: shop, transport, renting, work, doctor, office & karta pobytu, bank & post, school, everyday phrases and key verbs for the B1 exam. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, after the trial ends. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it any time in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Polski Krok → Settings → Restore purchases.
