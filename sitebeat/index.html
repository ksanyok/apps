How it works

Add your website and SiteBeat gets a pet that reflects its health: happy when the site is online and fast, sluggish when it is slow, worried when the SSL certificate expires in less than 14 days, and sick when the site is down. The app checks your sites when you open it and in the background; our server also checks them (every hour for free, every 5 minutes with Pro) and sends a notification when a site goes down or comes back. Allow notifications and Live Activities for SiteBeat in Settings to get alerts and the pet on your Lock Screen.

SiteBeat Pro

SiteBeat Pro is an auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly) with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. It lets you watch up to 10 sites, checks them from our server every 5 minutes and unlocks pet colors. Payment is charged to your Apple Account after the trial ends. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase, open SiteBeat → Settings → Restore purchases.
