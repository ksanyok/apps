Privacy Policy — SiteBeat

Last updated: October 27, 2026

SiteBeat: Website Pet & Uptime ("the app") is developed by Alexandr Krykun ("we"). We respect your privacy.

What the app sends to our server

To check your websites while your phone is asleep and to notify you, the app sends to our server (hosted on Cloudflare): the addresses of the websites you add, and the push notification tokens that Apple gives the app (one for alerts, one for the Lock Screen pet). Nothing else: no name, email, contacts, location, advertising identifier or account. These tokens cannot be used to identify you.

How we use it

Only to check those websites and send you notifications about them. We do not sell or share this data, do not use it for advertising and do not track you.

How long we keep it

As long as the website is on your list. Removing a site deletes it from our server; when you delete the app, Apple tells our server the token is no longer valid and we delete your list.

Data stored on your device

Your site list, check results and the pet's look are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Website checks

SiteBeat opens the websites you add like a regular visitor and measures how fast they answer. The SEO audit link opens promopilot.link in your browser; that website has its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
