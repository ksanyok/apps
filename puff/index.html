Puff: Lock Screen Pet

Support

How it works

Puff is a tiny pet that lives on your iPhone. Feed it, play with it and put it to sleep — in the app, or right from the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island after you tap "Show on Lock Screen". Live Activities must be allowed for Puff in Settings → Puff.

Puff Pro

Puff Pro is an auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly) with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. It unlocks 5 more friends, all colors and accessories. Payment is charged to your Apple Account after the trial ends. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase, open Puff → Settings → Restore purchases.
