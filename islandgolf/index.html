How it works

Every day there is a new 9-hole course, par 3 on every hole. You get one shot an hour. Tap Swing and a power bar starts filling; tap Hit when it reaches the flag. Too early lands short, too late flies long. Watch the wind, the water and the bunkers.

Tap “Play from the Lock Screen” in the app and the current hole appears on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island as a Live Activity, with the Swing and Hit buttons right there. iOS ends Live Activities after about 8 hours — open the app once and it comes back.

Free and Island Golf Pro

Free: the Meadow course every day. Island Golf Pro: five more courses (sea links, dunes, glacier, volcano, moon base), colorful balls and challenge links — send a friend your score and they play the same course on the same day.

Island Golf Pro is an auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly) with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, after the trial ends. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it any time in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase, open Island Golf → Settings → Restore purchases.

Your data

Your rounds and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
