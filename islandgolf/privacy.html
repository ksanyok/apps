Privacy Policy — Island Golf

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Island Golf: Lock Screen Golf ("the app") is developed by Alexandr Krykun ("we"). We respect your privacy.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, upload to our servers, share or sell personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your rounds, scores and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Challenge links

When you share a challenge, the link contains only the course, the date, your score and the name you typed. It is shared through the iOS share sheet by you; we do not receive it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
