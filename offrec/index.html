How it works

Tap the microphone and speak. Offrec writes down your words live, then turns them into a short summary with key points and a task list you can check off. Speech is transcribed on your iPhone with Apple's on-device speech recognition. Summaries are written on-device by Apple Intelligence on supported iPhones; on other iPhones Offrec picks the key sentences and tasks from your words. Say “Record in Offrec” to Siri or put Offrec on the Action Button via the Shortcuts app.

If your iPhone can't transcribe the chosen language offline, Offrec keeps the audio only — unless you turn on “Allow Apple speech servers” in Settings, in which case Apple's speech recognition service processes that audio under Apple's privacy policy.

Free and Offrec Pro

Free: recordings up to 3 minutes, 5 summaries a week, transcripts always. Offrec Pro: unlimited length and summaries, rewriting a note as an email or a post, search and export.

Offrec Pro is an auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly) with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, after the trial ends. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it any time in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase, open Offrec → Settings → Restore purchases.

Your data

Your recordings, transcripts and summaries are stored only on your device. Deleting a note deletes its audio; deleting the app deletes everything.
