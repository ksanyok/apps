Privacy Policy — Offrec

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Offrec: Private Voice Notes ("the app") is developed by Alexandr Krykun ("we"). We respect your privacy.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, upload to our servers, share or sell personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Microphone and speech recognition

The app uses the microphone only while you record. Audio is transcribed with Apple's speech recognition on your device. If your iPhone cannot transcribe the chosen language offline, the app keeps the audio without a transcript, unless you turn on “Allow Apple speech servers” in Settings; then the audio of that recording is processed by Apple's speech recognition service under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive your audio or text.

On-device AI

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, summaries are generated on your device with Apple's Foundation Models framework. No text is sent to us or to any server.

Data stored on your device

Recordings, transcripts, summaries and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
