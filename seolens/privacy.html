Privacy Policy — SEO Lens

Last updated: October 27, 2026

SEO Lens: Meta Tag Inspector ("the app") is developed by Alexandr Krykun ("we"). We respect your privacy.

Data we collect

None. The app and its Safari extension do not collect, store on any server, share or sell any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

How the extension works

The extension reads a web page only when you open SEO Lens on that page in Safari. The page is analysed on your device; the result is shown to you and, if you have Pro, saved in the app's history on your device.

Broken-link check (Pro)

When you tap "Check links", requests to the links of the current page go directly from your device to those websites, as if you opened them. We do not see or collect these requests or their results.

Full site audit

The "Full site audit" button opens audit.promopilot.link in Safari with the site's domain filled in. That website has its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
