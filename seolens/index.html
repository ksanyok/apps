How it works

SEO Lens is a Safari extension for iPhone. Open any website in Safari, tap aA in the address bar and choose SEO Lens: a report slides up over the page with the title and description (with length), H1–H3 headings, canonical, robots, hreflang, Open Graph and Google previews, images without alt text and nofollow links. Tap "Copy report" to share it, or "Full site audit" to run a free audit of the whole site on promopilot.link.

First time: in Safari tap aA → Manage Extensions → switch on SEO Lens and allow it.

SEO Lens Pro

SEO Lens Pro is an auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly) with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. It adds schema.org structured data, a broken-link check, history of checked pages and PDF export. Payment is charged to your Apple Account after the trial ends. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase, open SEO Lens → Settings → Restore purchases.
