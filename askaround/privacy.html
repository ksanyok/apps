Privacy Policy — Askaround

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Askaround: Party Questions ("the app") is developed by Alexandr Krykun ("we"). We respect your privacy.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Player names you type and your settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

On-device AI

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, new questions are generated on your device with Apple's Foundation Models framework. No text is sent to us or to any server.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
