How to play

Pick a pack, read the question on the card out loud and answer it, then tap Next (or swipe left) and pass the phone to the next person. Add players on the home screen to see whose turn it is. "New questions" adds fresh questions: on iPhones with Apple Intelligence they are written on your device by Apple's on-device model; otherwise all packs are reshuffled.

Askaround Pro

Askaround Pro is an auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly) with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. It unlocks the Deep talk, Couples, Friends and Hot takes packs and New questions. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, after the trial ends. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it any time in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Askaround → Settings → Restore purchases.
