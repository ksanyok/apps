How to play

Fill every cell with a sun or a moon. Each row and column holds as many suns as moons, no more than two of the same sit side by side, and no two rows or columns are identical. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

Evenly Pro

Evenly Pro is an auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly) with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. It unlocks daily 8×8 and 10×10 puzzles, endless mode, the full archive and unlimited hints. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, after the trial ends. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it any time in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Evenly → Settings → Restore purchases.
