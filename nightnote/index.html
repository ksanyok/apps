How it works

Tap + to write a dream down, choose how it felt and add tags. Nightnote recognises common dream symbols in your text and shows what they often mean. With Nightnote Pro you also get a reflection on each dream (written on-device by Apple Intelligence on supported iPhones, otherwise built from the symbol library), Insights, Face ID lock and export. Reflections are for self-reflection and fun — not predictions or medical advice. Turn on a morning reminder in Settings.

Nightnote Pro

Nightnote Pro is an auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly) with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, after the trial ends. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it any time in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Nightnote → Settings → Restore purchases.

Your data

Your journal is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your journal; use Export (Pro) to keep a copy.
